Prasanna Mayilvahanan

I'm a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, Germany. My research so far has been on pre-training and robustness in large-scale language and vision-language models. I'm currently exploring topics related to efficient exploration strategies for LLM reasoning, as well as understanding pre-training data characteristics that enhance reasoning abilities post-training.

Research

I'm interested in understanding and improving the capabilities of large-scale language and vision-language models, particularly in pre-training dynamics, robustness, and reasoning. Recent papers are highlighted.

LLMs on the Line: Data Determines Loss-to-Loss Scaling Laws
Prasanna Mayilvahanan, Thaddäus Wiedemer, Sayak Mallick, Matthias Bethge, Wieland Brendel
ICML, 2025
project page / arXiv

We find that loss-to-loss scaling laws depend on pretraining data and tokenizer, not model size, optimization, or even significant architectural differences.
